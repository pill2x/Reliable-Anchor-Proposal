Site Analysis

⚓

Reliable Anchor operates in a high-stakes, high-trust industry — customs clearance for real cargo, real money, and real regulatory consequences. The site is live, structured, and content-rich, but carries functional bugs and credibility gaps that any serious corporate importer would spot before making contact. The foundation is good; the execution needs finishing.

✓ What's already working

Strengths to build on

Well-structured service pages (5 dedicated pages with detailed copy), per-page FAQ sections, real named testimonials with company affiliations, a live blog with recent content, an Importer's Guide section (rare competitive asset), a working enquiry form with cargo-type selection, WhatsApp floating button, and a proper .ng domain. The site communicates expertise — it just doesn't quite earn trust yet.

Critical

All "All Services" sidebar links → 404

Every service subpage has a right sidebar listing all services (Custom Clearance, Compliance & Advisory, Port Operations, etc.) as clickable CTAs. Every single one of them returns a 404 error. This means any visitor browsing services hits a dead wall the moment they try to navigate. It's the most damaging functional bug on the site and needs to be the first thing fixed.

Critical

Gmail contact email on a licensed company

The header bar and contact section display anchorreliable32@gmail.com. In B2B customs clearance — where clients entrust you with six-figure cargo — a Gmail address is an immediate disqualifier for corporate procurement teams. A professional email at reliableanchor.ng must replace it.

Critical

All social media links are dead placeholders

Every social icon across the header, footer, and hero links to #. Confirmed visible across all service pages. A company with no working social presence appears unestablished or abandoned to any visitor who tries to verify it. Connect real profiles or remove the icons entirely.

Issue

Search bar buried in the service sidebar

A "Search Here" field appears only in the sidebar of service subpages — not in the main navigation. For a site with 8+ service pages, blog posts, and an Importer's Guide, site-wide search should live in the navbar where users expect to find it on any page.

Issue

Inconsistent and broken stat counters

The homepage shows "K+ Satisfied Customers" alongside real numbers like "25+ Experience" and "120+ Deliveries." The mixed formatting (some formatted, some broken template variables) makes the social proof section look unfinished and undermines the credibility the real numbers are meant to build.

Issue

No licence or regulatory credentials visible

The site repeatedly claims "licensed customs clearance" but displays no NCS licence number, CAC registration, or any verifiable accreditation. In an industry where fake clearing agents are a known risk, the absence of verifiable credentials is a significant red flag for serious importers conducting due diligence.

Issue

Duplicate heading text on the Compliance page

The Compliance & Trade Advisory service page contains a visible copy error: the heading reads "Why Choose Reliable Anchor Why Choose Reliable Anchor" — the phrase printed twice in the same line. Small errors like this signal a lack of attention to detail to any client proofreading the page before making a decision.

Issue

Service pages are template clones of each other

The Customs Clearance, Compliance & Advisory, and Freight & Logistics pages share identical layouts — same stock image arrangements, same red icon-box sections, same mirrored text grids. For a company with genuinely distinct services, this creates a poor impression: it looks like placeholder content rather than a differentiated offering, and makes it harder for visitors to understand what makes each service unique.

Opportunity

The Importer's Guide is an untapped lead-gen asset

The Importer's Guide is rare — very few Nigerian logistics companies offer structured educational content for importers. Currently it's a buried nav link. With an email gate for a downloadable PDF version, prominent homepage positioning, and cross-linking from blog posts and service pages, this single asset could become the site's most consistent source of high-intent, qualified leads. A serious first-time importer who finds and downloads this guide will call Reliable Anchor before anyone else.

Ten deliverables, sequenced by impact. D1–D3 are non-negotiable fixes — broken functionality and credibility errors that are actively costing enquiries today. D4–D10 are content, conversion, and positioning improvements that compound over time.

Deliverable Description Outcome
D1Fix all 404 service links
 Audit and repair every broken "All Services" sidebar link across all service subpages. Standardise all internal URLs to consistent clean paths. Set up 301 redirects from any old .html-extension URLs. Site is fully navigable

 Replace Gmail with info@reliableanchor.ng (or equivalent). Connect all social icons to real, active profiles — or remove them if profiles don't exist yet. These two fixes can be live within 24 hours of receiving access. Trust floor established
D3Licence & credentials section
 A dedicated "Accreditations" strip on the homepage and footer displaying NCS licence number, CAC registration, and any industry affiliations — formatted as recognisable badge-style visuals. B2B credibility signal
D4Navbar search integration
 Move site-wide search from the service sidebar into the main navigation bar. Removes the sidebar entirely or repurposes it as a cleaner service quick-links panel. Available on every page, on every device. Navigation & discoverability
D5Fix & unify stat counters
 Replace all broken "K+" placeholder values with real figures from the business. Apply consistent formatting across all counters. Add animated count-up on scroll for visual engagement. Social proof integrity
D6Importer's Guide lead capture
 Reposition the Importer's Guide as a homepage featured section. Gate a downloadable PDF version behind an email form. Integrate with Mailchimp or Brevo to build a warm lead list automatically. Lead generation engine

 Refine typography, tighten spacing, and elevate the visual identity toward the premium B2B end — without a full redesign. The site's red-and-green palette is workable; the execution needs polish and consistency. Perceived authority

 Break the identical template pattern across service pages. Each service gets a distinct visual section structure, unique hero framing, and service-specific supporting imagery — so the offering feels genuinely differentiated rather than copy-pasted. Clarity & brand depth

Phase 2 Vision — The Client Platform

Once the website is performing well, there is a significantly larger opportunity: converting reliableanchor.ng from a marketing site into an operational platform — a client-facing web app that manages the entire clearance journey digitally.

No Nigerian customs company currently offers this. The ones who build it first will own the category.

What a Reliable Anchor client portal would include

Phase 2 scope — proposed after Phase 1 delivery, based on results

📁 Client Document Portal

Clients upload Form M, Bill of Lading, PAAR, and other required documents directly through a secure dashboard — no more WhatsApp document chains.

📦 Shipment Status Tracker

Visual board showing real-time status: At Port → Customs Filing → Cleared → In Transit → Delivered. Clients check themselves; support calls drop significantly.

📋 Dynamic Document Checklist

Client selects cargo type and origin country — the system generates the exact required documents for that shipment. Eliminates the #1 cause of clearance delays.

💬 SMS / Email Alerts

"Your cargo has cleared customs." "Action needed: missing SONCAP certificate." Automated, timely notifications that make clients feel in control and reduce anxiety.

📊 Staff Admin Dashboard

Internal view: all active shipments, document status per client, outstanding actions, and client history — replacing the current WhatsApp and email chaos for the team.

💰 Quote Calculator

Public-facing tool: input cargo type, port of entry, and origin country — get an estimated timeline and cost range. Captures high-intent leads before competitors do.

Note: This is a Phase 2 proposal — shared here for strategic visibility, not as part of the current scope. Phase 1 fixes the foundation. Once results are visible, Phase 2 becomes the natural next conversation.
